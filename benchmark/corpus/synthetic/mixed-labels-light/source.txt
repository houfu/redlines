Section 1. Services

1.1 The Supplier shall provide the Services with reasonable skill and care.

1.2 The Supplier shall meet the service levels in Schedule 1.

(a) Availability is measured over each calendar month.

(b) Downtime for planned maintenance is excluded.

Section 2. Charges

2. The Customer shall pay the Charges monthly in arrears.

2.1 An invoice is due thirty days after receipt.
