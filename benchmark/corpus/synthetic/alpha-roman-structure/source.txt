7. PAYMENT

7.1 The Customer shall pay each invoice within thirty days of receipt.

(a) Invoices are issued monthly in arrears.

(b) Each invoice itemises the Services delivered in the period.

(c) The Supplier sends every invoice to the address in Schedule 1.

(d) The Customer may query an invoice within ten days of receipt.

(e) A queried line is set aside until the query is resolved.

(f) The rest of the invoice remains payable on the due date.

(g) Payment is made by bank transfer in pounds sterling.

(h) Each party bears its own banking charges.

(i) Interest accrues on any overdue amount at two per cent above base rate.

(j) The Supplier may suspend the Services while an amount is overdue.

7.2 Disputed invoices are handled as follows.

(i) The Customer notifies the Supplier within five Business Days.

(ii) The parties meet within ten Business Days of that notice.

(iii) A dispute that is still unresolved is escalated under clause 12.
