# Master Services Agreement

This Agreement is made between Acme Analytics Ltd and Beta
Retail plc.

## 3. Charges

3.1 The Client shall pay each invoice within sixty days of receipt.

The Supplier issues invoices monthly in arrears.

(a) Invoices are sent by email.

(b) Payment is made by bank transfer.

## 7. Termination

7.1 Either party may terminate this Agreement on thirty Business Days' written
notice.

7.2 The Supplier shall keep insurance appropriate to the Services in force throughout the Term and shall give evidence of it on request.

## Schedule 1
1. Any dispute arising out of this Agreement shall first be referred to a senior representative of each party, who shall meet within ten Business Days of the referral.
2. Support is provided during business hours.
