7. PAYMENT

7.1 The Customer shall pay each invoice within thirty Business Days of receipt.

(b) Invoices are issued monthly in arrears.

(c) Each invoice itemises the Services delivered in the period. The parties shall record any agreed change to this provision in writing.

(d) Any dispute arising out of this Agreement shall first be referred to a senior representative of each party, who shall meet within ten Business Days of the referral.

(e) The Supplier sends every invoice to the address in Schedule 1.

(f) The Customer may query an invoice within twenty days of receipt.

(g) A queried line is set aside until the query is resolved.

(h) The rest of the invoice remains payable on the due date.

(i) The parties meet within ten Business Days of that notice.

(j) Each party bears its own banking charges. Interest accrues on any overdue amount at two per cent above base rate.

(k) The Supplier is entitled to suspend the Services while an amount is overdue.

7.2 Disputed invoices are handled as follows.

(i) The Supplier notifies the Customer within five Business Days.

(iii) A dispute that is still unresolved is escalated under clause 12.
