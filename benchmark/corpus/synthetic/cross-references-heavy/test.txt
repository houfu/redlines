4. TERMINATION

4.1 Either party may terminate this agreement on sixty days' notice.

4.2 Each party shall keep records of the steps it takes under this Agreement and shall make those records available to the other party on reasonable written request.

4.3 Any dispute arising out of this Agreement shall first be referred to a senior representative of each party, who shall meet within ten Business Days of the referral.

4.4 Clause 4.1 is subject to clause 9.
