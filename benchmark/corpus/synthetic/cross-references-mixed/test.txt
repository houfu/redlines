4. TERMINATION

4.2 Either party may terminate this agreement on sixty days' notice.

4.3 The parties shall review the operation of this Agreement at least once in each year of the Term and shall record the outcome of that review in writing.

4.4 The preceding sub-clause does not apply during the Initial Term. This obligation survives the end of the Term.

4.5 Termination under the sub-clause above does not
affect accrued rights.
