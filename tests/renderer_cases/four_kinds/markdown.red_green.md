## 1. Delivery

1.1 The Supplier shall deliver the goods within <span style='color:red;font-weight:700;text-decoration:line-through;'>thirty (30</span><span style='color:green;font-weight:700;'>sixty (60</span>) days of each order.

<span style='color:red;font-weight:700;text-decoration:line-through;'>1.2 Risk in the goods passes to the Buyer when the goods are unloaded at the Buyer's warehouse.</span>

<span style='color:green;font-weight:700;'>1.2 The Buyer may reject goods that do not match the order.</span>

## 2. Payment

2.1 The Buyer shall pay each invoice within fourteen days of receiving it.

<span style='color:red;font-weight:700;text-decoration:line-through;'>2.2 Late payments carry interest at two per cent a month.</span>

<span style='color:green;font-weight:700;'>2.2 Risk in the goods passes to the Buyer when the goods are unloaded at the Buyer's warehouse.</span>
