Master Services Agreement

This Agreement is made on 1 March 2026 between Northwind Systems Limited, whose principal office is at 14 Harbour Row, Riverport (the "Supplier"), and Harbour Foods Group Limited, whose principal office is at 2 Meridian Way, Eastgate (the "Client").

recital 1. Background

recital 1.1 The Client operates a chain of grocery stores and wishes to procure the hosting and support of its online ordering platform.

recital 1.2 The Supplier supplies and supports software of that kind and has agreed to supply the Services to the Client.

recital 1.3 The parties have agreed to record in this Agreement the terms on which the Services are supplied.

definitions 2. Definitions

definition 2.1 "Agreement" means this document, its schedules and any statement of work agreed under it.

definition 2.2 "Business Day" means a day other than a Saturday, Sunday or public holiday on which banks are open for business in the place where the Services are supplied.

definition 2.3 "Charges" means the fees payable by the Client for the Services, calculated as set out in clause 5.

definition 2.4 "Confidential Information" means information disclosed by one party to the other in connection with this Agreement that is marked as confidential, or that the receiving party ought reasonably to treat as confidential.

definition 2.5 "Deliverable" means an item that the Supplier is to deliver to the Client under Schedule 1.

definition 2.6 "Service Level" means a target for the performance of the Services set out in Schedule 2.

definition 2.7 "Services" means the hosting, support and maintenance services described in Schedule 1.

definition 2.8 "Term" means the period for which this Agreement continues, as set out in clause 9.

3. Supply of the Services

clause 3.1 The Supplier shall supply the Services to the Client in accordance with this Agreement and Schedule 1.

clause 3.2 The Supplier shall supply the Services with reasonable skill and care, using personnel who are suitably qualified and experienced.

clause 3.3 The Supplier may engage a subcontractor to supply any part of the Services, and remains responsible for the acts and omissions of that subcontractor as if they were its own.

clause 3.33.4 The Supplier shall meet each Service Level and shall report on its performance against the Service Levels for each month within five Business Days of the end of that month.

clause 3.43.5 The Supplier may change the way in which the Services are supplied, provided that the change does not reduce their quality or availability.

4. Client Obligations

clause 4.1 The Client shall give the Supplier the access, information and co-operation that the Supplier reasonably needs in order to supply the Services.

clause 4.2 The Client shall keep the credentials issued to it secure and shall tell the Supplier promptly if it believes that they have been disclosed to anyone who is not authorised to use them.

clause 4.3 The Client is responsible for the accuracy of the data that it loads into the platform.

5. Charges and Payment

clause 5.1 The Client shall pay the Charges for the Services.

clause 5.2 The Charges are USD 12,000 for each month of the Term, and may be increased once in each year of the Term by no more than five per cent.

clause 5.3 The Supplier shall invoice the Charges monthly in arrears, and the Client shall pay each invoice within thirty days of receiving it.

clause 5.4 The Client may withhold payment of a disputed part of an invoice where each of the following applies:

sub_clause (a) The Client tells the Supplier of the dispute within ten Business Days of receiving the invoice.

sub_clause (b) The notice states the amount in dispute and the reason for it.

sub_clause (c) The Client pays the undisputed part of the invoice on the due date.

clause 5.5 Interest runs on any overdue amount at one per cent for each month from the due date until payment.

6. Intellectual Property

clause 6.1 Each party keeps the intellectual property rights that it owned before this Agreement began.

clause 6.2 The Supplier grants the Client a non-exclusive licence to use the platform and the Deliverables for the Term for the purposes of the Client's own business.

clause 6.3 The Client grants the Supplier a licence to use the Client's data to the extent needed to supply the Services.

7. Confidentiality

clause 7.1 Each party shall keep the other party's Confidential Information secret and shall use it only for the purposes of this Agreement.

clause 7.2 A party may disclose the other party's Confidential Information to those of its personnel and advisers who need it for those purposes, provided that it makes them aware of the obligations in clause 7.1.

clause 7.3 Clause 7.1 does not apply to information that is public otherwise than through a breach of this Agreement, or that the receiving party already held free of any duty of confidence.

clause 7.4 Neither party shall make a public announcement about this Agreement without the written consent of the other party.

8. Data Protection

clause 8.1 Each party shall comply with the data protection obligations that apply to it in respect of personal data processed under this Agreement.

clause 8.2 The Supplier shall not transfer personal data received from the Client to anyone else without the written instruction of the Client.

9. Term and Termination

clause 9.1 This Agreement begins on 1 April 2026 and continues for two years, unless it is terminated earlier under this clause.

clause 9.2 The Client may terminate this Agreement immediately by written notice if the Supplier fails to meet a Service Level in three consecutive months, contrary to clause 3.3.3.4.

clause 9.3 Either party may terminate this Agreement by giving the other no less than ninety days' written notice, expiring at the end of the Term or at any time after it.

clause 9.4 Either party may terminate this Agreement immediately by written notice if the other party commits a material breach that it does not remedy within thirty days of being asked in writing to do so.

clause 9.5 Termination does not affect any right or obligation that has accrued before it takes effect.

10. Liability

clause 10.1 Neither party excludes or limits its liability for fraud, or for anything else that cannot be limited under the law that applies to this Agreement.

clause 10.2 Neither party is liable to the other for loss of profit, loss of business or any indirect loss.

clause 10.3 The total liability of each party under this Agreement is limited to the Charges paid and payable in the twelve months before the event giving rise to the claim.

clause 10.4 Each party shall take reasonable steps to reduce any loss that it suffers.

11. General

clause 11.1 This Agreement is the entire agreement between the parties about its subject matter, and replaces any earlier agreement or understanding about that subject matter.

clause 11.2 A variation of this Agreement takes effect only if it is in writing and signed by both parties.

clause 11.3 Neither party may assign or transfer any of its rights or obligations under this Agreement without the written consent of the other party, which is not to be unreasonably withheld.

clause 11.4 If any provision of this Agreement is unenforceable, the rest of it continues in force.

clause 11.5 A notice given under this Agreement must be in writing and sent to the address of the receiving party set out above, or to any other address that the party notifies to the other in writing.

clause 11.6 A person who is not a party to this Agreement has no right to enforce it.

schedule Schedule 1. The Services

schedule 1. Hosting: the Supplier shall host the Client's online ordering platform on infrastructure that the Supplier manages.

schedule 2. Support: the Supplier shall answer questions from the Client's staff by email and by telephone on each Business Day.

schedule 3. Maintenance: the Supplier shall apply patches and updates to the platform, and shall keep a record of every change that it makes.

schedule The Supplier shall deliver the Deliverables set out below by the dates shown.

schedule The onboarding plan is due by Week 2 and is owned by the Supplier.

schedule The data migration is due by Week 6 and is owned by the Supplier.

schedule The test report is due by Week 8 and is owned by the Supplier.

schedule The training day is due by Week 9 and is owned by the Client.

schedule The go-live sign-off is due by Week 10 and is owned by the Client.

schedule 1. The Supplier shall acknowledge a priority 1 incident within thirty minutes and shall restore the Services within four hours.

schedule 2. The Supplier shall acknowledge a priority 2 incident within one hour and shall restore the Services within eight hours.

schedule 3. The Supplier shall acknowledge a priority 3 incident within four two hours and shall restore the Services within two one Business Days.Day.

schedule 4. The Supplier shall acknowledge a priority 4 incident within one Business Day and shall restore the Services within five Business Days.

schedule 5. The Supplier shall make the platform available for 99.5 per cent of each month, measured outside the maintenance window.

schedule 6. The Supplier shall apply a critical security patch within two Business Days of its release.

schedule 7. The Supplier shall apply any other security patch within ten Business Days of its release.

schedule 8. The Supplier shall give the Client a report on its performance against each Service Level for each month.
